Apollo 15: preserve the conversation

A listening review of tonal and broadband reduction across the delivered tapes.

How to review these samples

Start with the original, then switch treatments during a phrase. Check faint words, consonants, overlapping voices, and phrase endings. Use headphones at a comfortable, consistent level. Prefer the least processing that improves understanding.

Listen to removed signal to hear what a treatment subtracts. Recognizable words or voice texture there are evidence to examine against the original; they are not an automatic verdict. Rate preservation and noise separately. Mark a sample as speech, uncertain, or no useful speech.
